# Lumora

Light. Tone. Precision.

A personal photo editor for iPhone that runs in Safari: JPG and HEIC, light and colour adjustments, presets, crop, export. English by default, Spanish on the EN | ES switch. No account, no cloud: photos and edits stay on the phone.

Live: https://whitemex956.github.io/lumora/ (open in Safari, Share > Add to Home Screen).
